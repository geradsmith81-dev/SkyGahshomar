SKY GAHSHOMAR - the Zoroastrian calendars from NASA JPL astronomy
=================================================================

WHAT IT DOES
  * Shows the Shahanshahi, Kadmi, Fasli and Iranian Bastani calendars, plus a Sasanian
    reconstruction for comparison: today's roj, mah and year, the current Gah, festivals, the
    Moon and the Mah Niyayesh, and a "Face the light" compass toward the Sun or the Moon.
  * Works out sunrise, noon, sunset and the five Gahs for your place from the NASA JPL DE440s
    ephemeris (1850-2149). Your place is found by the browser (it asks once), chosen from presets,
    or typed in.

FIRST START (Windows)
  1. Python 3.12 or newer must be installed once (tested with 3.15):
     https://www.python.org/downloads/  -  on the first installer screen tick "Add python.exe to PATH".
  2. Double-click "Start app.bat".
     The first time, a setup window installs the app's parts (2-5 minutes, needs internet). It
     installs only the exact versions Sky Gahshomar was tested with, and checks each download
     against its published checksum. After you unzip a newer version of Sky Gahshomar, the same
     window appears once more to bring the parts up to date.
  3. The app opens in its own window (address http://localhost:8513). There is no black window
     any more: Sky Gahshomar runs quietly in the background while its window is open.
  4. At the end of the first-time setup a Sky Gahshomar icon is put on your Desktop and in the
     Start menu. If it is missing (or you move the folder), double-click "Create desktop icon.bat".

STOPPING THE APP
  * Close the Sky Gahshomar window. The app stops by itself about a minute and a half later.
  * Or open "This computer: quitting Sky Gahshomar" at the bottom of the page and press
    "Quit Sky Gahshomar" to stop it at once.
  * Starting Sky Gahshomar while it is already running simply brings up its window again.
  * Sky Gahshomar only accepts connections from this computer. Other devices on the same network
    cannot open it unless you start "Share on this network.bat".

SHARING ON YOUR NETWORK (optional)
  * Double-click "Share on this network.bat". A window shows the address to open on a phone,
    tablet or another computer on the same Wi-Fi, and a password that is new every time.
  * If Windows Firewall asks, allow Python on PRIVATE networks only. Do not share on public
    Wi-Fi (cafes, hotels, conferences, campus guest networks).
  * Visitors must enter the password. Only someone at this computer sees the Quit button.
  * Close that window to stop sharing (this also stops the app).
  * The password keeps casual visitors out; it is not a substitute for a trusted network.

WHERE YOUR DATA IS KEPT
  * Sky Gahshomar writes only to your user folder, never to the program folder:
      %LOCALAPPDATA%\SkyGahshomar\ephem   a replacement NASA JPL ephemeris (see below)
      %LOCALAPPDATA%\SkyGahshomar\data    a rebuilt table of equinoxes and solstices (see below)
      %LOCALAPPDATA%\SkyGahshomar\logs    what the app reported while running (last four runs)
    (paste %LOCALAPPDATA%\SkyGahshomar into the File Explorer address bar to open it).
  * The ephem and data folders normally do not exist: the program folder already holds both files
    (ephem\de440s.bsp and data\seasons.csv). They are made only if one of those files goes missing.
    You may delete them at any time.
  * Sky Gahshomar stores no personal data. Your place is used only while its window is open and is
    never written to disk.

IF SKY GAHSHOMAR SAYS ITS FILES HAVE CHANGED
  * At every start Sky Gahshomar checks its program files against the list in MANIFEST.sha256. If a
    file is missing or different (deleted by accident, edited, or damaged), it says which ones.
    The safest fix is to download Sky Gahshomar again and unzip a fresh copy over the old folder
    (your data in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SkyGahshomar is not affected). This check catches accidents; it is
    not protection against someone deliberately altering the program.
  * If the app ever fails to start, the reason is in
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\SkyGahshomar\logs\skygahshomar.log.

CHECKING YOUR DOWNLOAD
  * Each release lists the SHA-256 checksum of SkyGahshomar_desktop.zip (in SHA256SUMS.txt). To
    check a download, open PowerShell in the download folder and run:
        Get-FileHash SkyGahshomar_desktop.zip -Algorithm SHA256
    The long number it prints must match the one published with the release.

USING IT
  * Choose a calendar and a page along the top. "Settings: tradition, place, festivals" holds the
    community presets, your place and the festival options.
  * The Compare page shows all five calendars side by side; the other pages show one.
  * HOW_IT_WORKS.md explains the rules and their sources.
